# Status

- **Version:** 0.1.0-candidate, 1 November 2026. Tag `v0.1.0-candidate`, which will never be moved.
- **Status:** unrefereed research candidate. Internal AI-assisted review only; not peer-reviewed, independently reproduced or formally verified.
- **DOI:** 10.5281/zenodo.23856194 (concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23856193).
- **Proved in the manuscript (written proofs):** Theorem 1 (spectral Riccati envelope for a weighted average of $h(x_i)=-x_i-\log(1-x_i)$ under a quadratic-form condition, transferred to the weighted average of $x_i$ by Jensen's inequality) and Corollary 2 (when the condition holds: symmetric, temporal, heterogeneous-recovery and Perron-weighted directed cases); Theorem 3 (Conjecture 1 of Persoons, Sensi, Prasse and Van Mieghem 2024: $y(t)\le1/(1+t)$ at or below threshold); Theorem 4 (Conjecture 2, with prefactor $1-\bar p$ and an exponential form); Proposition 5 (attainment on regular graphs) and Proposition 6 (exact subcritical rate); Theorem 7 and Corollary 8 (critical constant: $t\,x_i(t)\to v_i/\sum_j u_j\delta_j v_j^2$, $\kappa(G)\le1$ with equality iff regular, $r\,T(r)\to\kappa(G)$); Proposition 9 (node-wise bound $\sqrt{n/(t(1+t))}$ and asymptotic worst-node order $n^{1/3}$); Proposition 10 (counterexamples to stronger statements); Propositions 11 and 12 (SIR/SIRS; expected prevalence of the exact stochastic SIS process with a common recovery rate, via Simon and Kiss 2018); Proposition 13 and Theorem 14 (mixed initial states; unconditional transition-time bounds for all $R_0$).
- **Computed, not proved:** the values of $\kappa$, transition times and node constants on synthetic networks (Tables 1, 1b, 2; Figure 1); the two-solver numerical finding that the growth formula of Persoons et al. (their eq. 20) under-estimates growth times within its hypotheses away from $R_0\to1$ (Table 3; high-precision integration with two solvers, not interval-certified).
- **Not claimed:** a sharp growth bound on heterogeneous graphs; the uniform-in-time node-wise order; scaling laws for $\kappa$; anything about a particular real epidemic or real contact network; stochastic bounds beyond the upper bound for a common recovery rate.
